Features

Non-Destructive Editing

Edit your photos without permanently changing the original files.

Designed to run like a Game

Wena will sync to your monitor refresh rate no glitching no stuttering smooth silky performance

Modern UI/UX

A different UI/UX that you have not seen in other photo editing software till now, Wena starts clean everytime no boilerplate of your workspace add what you need to each edit

Advanced Color Grading

Professional color tools including curves, and tone mapping, high precision histogram and many more features.

All hardware support

No matter you have a High-end PC or an old PC Wena is designed to be performant base on your hardware specifications

Fast Export

Wena is designed to export bulk photos in no time

Built in high precision Film(Negative) color grading process

By one click you can colorize your old film photos

Raw, Tiff, JPEG, HEIC, HEIF Support

Import and process RAW files from modern cameras with full precision.

32 bit float precision

All the calculations are done in 32-bit floats

Cross-platform

Available for Gnu/Linux, Windows and soon will be available for MacOS as well.
