Powerful Features for Professionals

Everything you need to bring your creative vision to life

Non-Destructive Editing

Edit your photos without permanently changing the original files. Every adjustment is reversible.

Game-Engine Performance

Syncs to your monitor's refresh rate for buttery-smooth, glitch-free editing experience.

Modern UI/UX

Clean interface that starts fresh every time. Add tools as needed for each unique edit.

Advanced Color Grading

Professional tools including curves, tone mapping, and high-precision histograms.

Instant RAW Preview

Lightning-fast embedded preview extraction lets you browse thousands of RAW files instantly without waiting for full processing.

One-Click Pipeline Copy

Perfect your edit once, then instantly apply the same settings to other images. Maintain consistency across entire photo series effortlessly.

Universal Hardware Support

Optimized for high-end PCs and older hardware—adapts to your system's capabilities.

Lightning-Fast Export

Bulk export hundreds of photos with optimized performance and minimal wait time.

Built-in high-precision tools to colorize vintage film and negative photos with one click.

Universal Format Support

32-Bit Float Precision

All calculations performed in 32-bit floating point for maximum color accuracy.
