Features

Non-Destructive Editing

Edit your photos without permanently changing the original files.

Designed to Run Like a Game

Wena syncs to your monitor's refresh rate for smooth, glitch-free performance.

Modern UI/UX

A unique interface that starts clean every time—no cluttered workspace. Add tools as needed for each edit.

Advanced Color Grading

Professional tools including curves, tone mapping, high-precision histograms, and more.

All Hardware Support

Optimized for high-end PCs or older hardware—Wena adapts to your system's specifications.

Fast Export

Export bulk photos in no time with optimized performance.

Built-in High-Precision Film (Negative) Color Grading

Colorize your old film photos with one click.

Raw, TIFF, JPEG, HEIC, HEIF Support

Import and process RAW files from modern cameras with full precision.

32-Bit Float Precision

All calculations are performed in 32-bit floats for maximum accuracy.

Cross-Platform

Available for GNU/Linux, Windows, and soon macOS.
